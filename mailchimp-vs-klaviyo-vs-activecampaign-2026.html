13. Migration Guide

Migrating is scary, but worth it. Here's how to do it without losing revenue.

Week 1: Preparation

  • Export subscriber list (CSV) from old platform
  • Clean your list: remove bounces, unsubscribes, inactive (180+ days)
  • Document your current automations (screenshots)
  • Set up new account (don't cancel old one yet!)

Week 2: Setup & Testing

  • Import cleaned subscribers
  • Install Shopify/WooCommerce integration
  • Recreate top 3 automations (abandoned cart, welcome, post-purchase)
  • Set up email authentication (DKIM, SPF, DMARC)
  • Send test campaigns to small segment (500 people)

Week 3: Parallel Testing

  • Run both platforms simultaneously
  • Compare deliverability, opens, clicks
  • Monitor revenue from each platform
  • Fix any issues before full migration

Week 4: Full Migration

  • Turn off automations on old platform
  • Turn on all automations on new platform
  • Monitor closely for 7 days
  • Keep old platform active for 30 days (safety net)

Pro Tips:

  • Migrate during slow season (NOT before Black Friday!)
  • Use same "From Name" and "From Email" for deliverability
  • Budget 2 weeks of potentially 10-15% lower performance during transition
  • Don't migrate ESP and rebrand at the same time (too risky)

What We Lost During Migration: When we migrated from Mailchimp to Klaviyo, open rates dropped 15% for 2 weeks while Gmail/Yahoo learned to trust the new sending infrastructure. We lost ~$8K in revenue during transition. But within 30 days, we were generating $15K MORE per month. Worth it.
